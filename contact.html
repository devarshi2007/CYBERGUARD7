Frequently Asked Questions

What is this cybersecurity platform about?

This platform provides educational resources, tools, and AI-powered guidance to help users understand cybersecurity concepts and stay protected from digital threats.

Who is this website designed for?

Our platform is designed for students, beginners, tech enthusiasts, and anyone interested in improving their cybersecurity awareness and digital safety.

What kind of cybersecurity topics do you cover?

We cover a wide range of topics including cybersecurity fundamentals, phishing and malware awareness, network security basics, security tools, and industry frameworks.

What is Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and why is it essential?

Explaining why passwords alone are no longer enough to secure sensitive accounts.

No. This platform focuses on cybersecurity education and awareness. It does not provide real-time monitoring or direct protection services.
