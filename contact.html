Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you respond to security incidents?

Our emergency hotline is available 24/7 with response times under 1 hour for critical incidents. MDR clients have dedicated contact channels with even faster escalation.

Do you offer services for small businesses?

Yes! We have scalable solutions for small businesses including affordable penetration testing, security training, and vCISO services tailored to smaller budgets.

What's included in the free consultation?

The 30-minute consultation covers your security goals, current challenges, and a preliminary recommendation for services. You'll receive a custom proposal with no obligation.

How do you ensure confidentiality during assessments?

All assessments are covered by NDA and our team signs confidentiality agreements. We follow industry standards for data protection and never share findings without authorization.

Can we discuss pricing before scheduling a call?

Pricing varies based on scope and organization size. Email sales@cyberguard.io with details about your needs and we'll provide a preliminary estimate before your call.
